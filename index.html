Welcome

Welcome to ISKCON Halifax

✦

A Vedic Culture Centre dedicated to sharing the timeless wisdom of the Bhagavad-Gita and fostering a community of devotion, compassion, and spiritual growth in Halifax, Nova Scotia.

Caring

In a chaotic, modern world, ISKCON of Halifax provides an oasis that serves to nourish the spiritual, cultural, emotional, and educational needs of its members.

Inspiring

By following the pure tenets laid down by Lord Krishna in Bhagavad-Gita, families cultivate a deep understanding of these teachings and their practical application in daily life.

Maintaining

A center dedicated to perpetuating the pure principles of bhakti-yoga in direct line with the past saintly personalities of the Vedic tradition, relevant for generations to come.

Sacred Purpose

Incalculable Spiritual Merits of Building a Temple

✦

The Bhagavata Purana states that one who offers gifts to facilitate the worship of Lord Krishna's deity within a temple will receive an opulence equal to the Lord himself.

Invest in your eternal spiritual future by participating in this auspicious undertaking to build a temple of Lord Krishna in Halifax.

Our Journey

Current Facility

✦

Halifax International Society for Krishna Consciousness is a registered charity organization since 2007. Currently, we organize our weekly programs in a rental space at Centennial Arena, 27 Vimy Ave.

Over the past 3 years alone, our cultural festivals have attracted over 12,000 visitors from the Maritimes, reflecting the community's growth and enthusiasm.

However, the existing rental space increasingly limits functionality and regular programs, creating a dire need for a bigger and permanent facility. A permanent temple is essential for providing a dedicated space for daily worship, festivals, community outreach, and educational programs.
