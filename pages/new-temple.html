"A person who builds a temple of Sri Vishnu will get the same results as performing the Rajasuya Yajna and Ashvamedha Yajna. Oh best of the brahmanas, as many atoms are there in the temple so built, for so many thousands of years will he be able to reside in the heavenly planets."

— Vishnu Dharmottara Purana

Our Future Home

New Temple Location

After comprehensive research, our management has identified a 7,700 sqft property on 9 acres of land, located at 29 Westwood Blvd, Upper Tantallon, Nova Scotia.

  • Easily accessible with 20-25 min drive time
  • Serene community environment
  • Near shops and parks
  • Serves growing Indian and multicultural population
Project Details

Temple Highlights

The new property will host a wide array of facilities designed to serve our diverse and growing community.

Spacious Darshan Hall

A magnificent 3,500 sqft space dedicated to worship and meditation.

Community Hall & Auditorium

A large gathering space for festivals, cultural events, and discourses.

Food For Life Counter

Distributing free sanctified vegetarian meals to those in need.

GOVINDA's Restaurant

Enjoy pure vegetarian, cruelty-free dining options.

Educational Facilities & Library

A treasure trove of Vedic literature and learning resources.

Vedic Books & Gifts Boutique

Offering spiritual literature, clothing, and devotional items.

Ashram & Boat Festivals Facility

Dedicated facility for resident monks and unique water festivals.

Yoga & Meditation Center

Serene spaces for yoga asanas, kirtan, and mantra meditation.

Parking for 80+ Vehicles

Ample parking space to accommodate our growing congregation.

Beautiful Landscaping

Lush gardens providing a tranquil and spiritual ambiance.

Vedic Sunday School Rooms

Dedicated rooms to nurture the younger generation in Vedic culture.

Playground for Children

A safe and fun outdoor environment for kids to enjoy.
