Welcome

Your Krishna Community

The International Society for Krishna Consciousness (ISKCON) has a 50-year history of sharing the glorious Vedic culture of India with people of all nations, races, and walks of life throughout the world. Founded in 1966 by His Divine Grace A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada, ISKCON is a movement with a mission to promote the well-being of society by teaching the science of Krishna consciousness according to Bhagavad-Gita and other ancient scriptures. ISKCON has over 600 centers, rural communities, schools, and restaurants across the globe.

Krishna Consciousness, also known as Bhakti Yoga, is the spiritual practice of reawakening our pure, original relationship with God (Krishna) through devotional service. It emphasizes chanting the holy names, studying authorized scriptures, and engaging in practical acts of love and devotion.

Our Core Focus

Our Mission

Our mission is to help people uncover their spiritual potential by guiding them on the path of loving devotion to Lord Krishna.

1

Chant

To chant the holy names of God, particularly the Hare Krishna maha-mantra, to cleanse the heart and awaken spiritual consciousness.

2

Read Srimad Bhagavatam

To systematically propagate spiritual knowledge to society at large and educate all people in the techniques of spiritual life.

3

Honor Prasadam

To distribute sanctified food (prasadam) to the public, purifying body and soul and spreading joy and good health.

What We Stand For

Our Values

Equal Vision (Sama Darshana)

Recognizing that all living beings are spiritual souls, parts and parcels of God, regardless of race, gender, or species.

Without Cruelty (Ahimsa)

Living compassionately and minimizing harm to others, which includes practicing a vegetarian or vegan lifestyle.

Humility (Amanitva)

Cultivating a humble state of mind, avoiding the desire for prestige, and offering respect to all others.

Choice (Iccha)

Respecting the free will of every individual to choose their own spiritual path and pace of advancement.

Nurturing Right Action (Dharma)

Acting responsibly in ways that sustain personal well-being, community harmony, and spiritual growth.

Affection (Prithi)

Fostering deep, loving relationships centered on our shared connection with the Supreme Lord.
