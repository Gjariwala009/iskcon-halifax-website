Our Beautiful Location

Our vibrant spiritual community is centered around a spacious 7,700 sqft facility set on 9 acres of beautiful, serene land. It provides a peaceful retreat from the hustle and bustle of city life, creating the perfect atmosphere for meditation, spiritual learning, and community gatherings.

Despite the tranquil setting, we are conveniently accessible, located just a 20-25 minute drive from downtown Halifax. Ample parking is available on site for all our guests and congregation members.

What to Expect When You Visit

✦

Whether you are attending a Sunday Feast, coming for kirtan, or visiting for the first time, our temple is open to all seekers of spiritual peace.

Devotional Darshan

Sacred Altar & Darshan

Experience peaceful darshan before the radiant deities of Sri Sri Gaura Nitai and offer your heartfelt prayers.

Kirtan & Discourses

Prayer & Meditation Hall

Join in congregational mantra meditation, soul-stirring kirtan with mridanga drums and kartalas, and Vedic discourses.

Free Sunday Feast

Sunday Prasadam Feast

Every Sunday Feast program concludes with a sumptuous, sanctified vegetarian dinner served to all guests with love.
